    The hard lines

    Four promises that don't bend for a vote, a sponsor or a good week.

    1. No money, ever.

      The Flock never takes deposits, payments or donations to invest. It never trades for anyone and never links to a broker, exchange or wallet. If someone says they speak for the Flock and asks you for money, that is a scam.

    2. Research, not advice.

      The portfolio is a paper model, the same for every reader. It knows nothing about your situation, and nothing here says what you should buy or sell. Results will be shown honestly, losses included, next to a plain index fund (VTI), even when the fund wins.

    3. Public information only.

      Built on public information: filings, earnings calls, government data, published research and news. No private tips, ever. Contributions must link the public sources they use.

    4. Built so it can't be pumped.

      Fixed rules set the weights, not opinions or popularity. Only large, liquid holdings: no penny stocks, memecoins, leverage, shorting or single-name options. Changes happen only when the research changes, and every one is logged with the reason.
