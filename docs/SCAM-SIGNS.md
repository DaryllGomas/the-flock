# Scam signs: the DM "investment group"

A message like this is the usual opening:

> "Hey, btw, I recently joined a great investment group. It's mostly made up of ordinary investors who share
> market updates … If you're interested, I can invite you to join. Don't feel pressured …"

How it usually goes, as regulators and the FBI describe it:
1. **A friendly stranger** (or a hacked friend's account) mentions a group casually, with no pressure.
2. **A group chat** (WhatsApp, Telegram) with a "professor" or "teacher" and many happy "members", most of them
   fake accounts run by the same people.
3. **Free tips that seem to work**, to build trust.
4. **A special app or platform** to follow the trades. The balance there goes up. It isn't real.
5. **Withdrawals fail**: there's a "tax", a "fee" or a "minimum" first. The money is gone.

Signs, any one of which is enough to walk away:
- Someone you don't know brings up investing out of nowhere.
- A private group, a "teacher", or "insider" or "guaranteed" tips.
- Being told to use an app or site you've never heard of, or to pay in crypto or by wire.
- Pressure that says it isn't pressure.

The Flock never DMs anyone, never has a private group and never asks for money. Report scams at reportfraud.ftc.gov
and ic3.gov (US).
